# Change Control

## Protected documents

- `00_constitution/CONSTITUTION.md`
- `01_vision/VISION.md`

## Change categories

### Minor documentation change

Examples:

- typo;
- formatting;
- clarification without semantic change.

### Major documentation change

Examples:

- new system;
- changed architecture;
- changed milestone;
- changed validation criteria.

### Vision amendment

Examples:

- changed product goal;
- changed target users;
- changed success criteria;
- changed non-goals.

## Approval rules

- Minor changes: one reviewer.
- Major changes: owner approval.
- Vision amendments: explicit human approval and audit.

## AI role

AI may draft change proposals but must not approve or directly apply protected changes.
