## Repository role
This repository maintains the Intent Preservation System (IPS): a documentation-first governance and tooling framework for AI-assisted delivery.

## Stakeholders
- Repository owner and maintainers approving governance changes.
- Adopting repositories consuming IPS standards, templates, and validators.

## Value delivered
- Protects original intent through immutable constitution and vision artifacts.
- Defines reusable traceability, planning, and validation contracts.
- Provides validator and audit tooling for adoption and delivery discipline.

## Out of scope in this repository
- No production runtime service is operated from this repository.
- No deployment/runtime ownership for other ecosystem services.
- No replacement of project-specific business intent in adopting repositories.
